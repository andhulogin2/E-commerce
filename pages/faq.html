Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping delivers in 2–4 business days across India. Express delivery options are available at checkout for urgent orders. Orders placed before 2 PM IST ship out the same day.
Once your order ships, you'll receive an SMS and email notification with a direct tracking link. You can also view live tracking updates anytime on your My Orders page.

Returns & Refunds

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all items. Items must be in original condition with tags intact. Returns are free — generate a prepaid return pickup directly from your Orders Page.
Once we receive your returned item, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method (or instantly as NovaCart Wallet credit, if selected).
