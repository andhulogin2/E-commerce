Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when placing an order (name, email, shipping address, payment details) and technical metadata (browser type, IP address, device specs) to personalize your shopping experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to process orders, fulfill shipments, send order status alerts, prevent fraud, and provide customer support. We never sell your personal data to third-party brokers.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain cart contents, session state, and user theme preferences (Dark/Light mode). Optional analytics cookies help us optimize site performance.

4. Data Protection & Security

All sensitive transactions are encrypted via 256-bit TLS protocols. Payment information is securely processed through certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.
