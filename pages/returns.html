Returns & Shipping Policy

Updated 2026 Policy Standards

1. 30-Day Risk-Free Returns

We want you to love everything you buy from NovaCart. If you are not 100% satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

2. How to Initiate a Return

1. Go to your Orders Page.
2. Click "Request Return" on the delivered order.
3. Print your complimentary prepaid return shipping label and attach it to your package.
4. Drop off the box at any authorized carrier drop-point.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your return is received at our distribution hub, inspection takes 1–2 business days. Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
